Holds a BA in Social Sciences, a PhD in Sociology, and a postdoctoral degree in Social History from the University of São Paulo, with a doctoral exchange at the École des Hautes Études en Sciences Sociales in Paris. He is an Associate Professor in the Department of Social Sciences at ICH/UFJF.

He coordinated UFJF’s Graduate Program in Social Sciences, served as executive director of Editora UFJF, and was Assistant Editor for Sociology at the Brazilian Journal of Social Sciences. He is currently executive editor of Revista Teoria e Cultura (PPGCSO/UFJF).

He leads the CNPq research group “Capitalism, Collapse and Utopia” and is an associated researcher at, among others, the Digital Humanities Laboratory at UFBA. His work covers sociological theory, social and critical theory, homelessness, popular music, cultural industries, artists, and intellectuals.
